# 📖 Introduction

Tracking technologies have become essential for security, logistics, and daily object management.

This project evaluates **three affordable tracking systems** and compares them with premium market options such as:

- Apple AirTag  
- Samsung SmartTag2  
- Tile Pro  

The goal is to determine the best alternatives under Colombian market conditions.
